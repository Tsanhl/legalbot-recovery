## Purpose and limits

Test whether a person with a real-world legal problem can understand their position
and take the next appropriate step. Questions start with situations and documents,
not a supplied statutory extract chosen because it is easy to answer. The earlier
23/23 supplied-evidence result does not establish retrieval, upload handling,
case-law selection, practical case management or comprehensive everyday coverage.
The upcoming run is a scoped experimental, scenario-first Codex diagnostic
workflow. It is not a product deployment certification or professional assurance.

The bot should provide careful legal analysis and practical assistance in plain
language. It must not pretend to be a solicitor, claim representation, guarantee
success, submit a claim or send correspondence without the applicable authority.
There is no finite bank that covers every possible question or all legal systems.
This bank measures limited UK/USA samples. Location and issue-specific applicability
must distinguish England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, and USA federal
and state law. It cannot prove coverage of all legal areas in every state or UK
jurisdiction. Explicitly route or defer uncertain jurisdiction, other countries,
US territories, tribal law and unsupported law.

## Expected assistance

1. Identify jurisdiction, relevant dates, parties and the user's objective. Ask
   only for facts that change the answer, and give safe conditional guidance now
   when possible rather than a generic request for more information.
2. Read the uploaded evidence, with exact document/page references. Distinguish
   an assertion, an observation, a disputed fact and a verified legal proposition.
3. Identify rights, duties, material conditions/exceptions and realistic remedies.
   Apply them to the facts, including the other side's strongest material argument.
4. Retrieve applicable legislation and procedure independently from official
   authorities for the stated location. Check extent, commencement/effective date,
   amendments/effects and source availability; determine USA federal/state
   applicability per issue. A UK-only source route does not establish USA coverage.
5. Use case law when a verified holding affects the issue. Check exact case identity,
   court, paragraph, binding/persuasive status, later treatment and factual analogy.
   An unexplained citation list, an irrelevant famous case or an invented quotation
   fails. A statute-led answer can be complete without a case; require a recorded
   relevance assessment instead of an arbitrary case-law quota.
6. Give ordered next steps: evidence to preserve, the appropriate recipient,
   remedy request, complaint/ADR/ombudsman/court route where applicable, deadlines
   with their trigger and calculation, costs, risks and escalation conditions.
7. Provide a useful draft complaint or response when appropriate. Dates, facts,
   amounts and demanded remedies must be evidence-bound. Drafting does not send
   or file it, and the bot must never claim that it has contacted anyone.
8. Use deterministic citations from reviewed metadata. Give proportionate urgent
   help/referral where the facts require it. State material uncertainty honestly.

## Exposed consumer example — UK illustration only

The owner's example of a parent buying a defective product and being refused a
refund is a visible requirement, not an unseen question. The custodian must not
copy it, lightly paraphrase it or count it as newly unseen. The broader faulty-goods
family remains eligible for independently designed private scenarios. The CRA
checklist below illustrates UK analysis only; it supplies no USA rule. A USA
consumer scenario needs independently researched applicable federal/state law.

The acceptance checklist includes jurisdiction; trader/private seller/marketplace
identity; the buyer and any agency issue; purchase/delivery/rejection dates; product
description, fault and use; receipt and alternative purchase proof; price/payment
method; previous repair/replacement attempts; retailer messages; and the desired
outcome. Receipt of an upload never proves the alleged defect or establishes that
the uploaded statements are true.

For the UK illustration, the legal oracle must assess the relevant Consumer Rights
Act 2015 goods provisions including sections 9, 19, 20, 22, 23 and 24 as applicable, instead of assuming every
fault or time period automatically produces the same refund. A warranty does not
replace statutory rights. Cancellation, statutory rejection, repair/replacement,
price reduction, burden of proof and limitation are distinct issues. Credit-card
or payment-service routes must be assessed only when their conditions are met.
The action plan should specify what to ask the seller for, evidence to attach,
an appropriate response period, and a proportionate escalation route. Case law is
used only when independently verified and materially relevant. No actual family
case has been assessed and no family document has been received in this request.

Reference entry points: [Consumer Rights Act 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/contents)
and [GOV.UK returns guidance](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds).
Guidance is an orientation source, not a substitute for controlling provisions.
These entry points do not establish currentness for the future private bank.
