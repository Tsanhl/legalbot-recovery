# Evaluation, improvement and training workflow

## Approved preparation policy

On 1 September 2026, the owner approved:

1. factual-first evaluation of all 331 visible GE cases, with 32 system scenarios
   reported separately and no unseen use;
2. a hard factual/legal gate followed by practical GE or adapted 70+ PB/Essay
   quality review, delivered as readable answers and one owner approval DOCX; and
3. diagnosis and non-weight improvement after baseline, with weight training kept
   separate and visible re-evaluation returned before any unseen run; and
4. a GE-first closed loop that preserves the 331 principal cases and separate 32
   system scenarios, adds only separately identified visible diagnostic questions
   for demonstrated missing-area coverage, and repeats the full visible suite until
   the exact exit gate passes or the repeated-failure stop rule fires.

This policy does not supply the exact sources/currentness, gold, model transport,
resources, private roots or run authorization. No run starts until those applicable
inputs pass.

## Owner experience

The owner reviews readable answers rather than raw gate logs. Each review shows:

- question and declared mode/jurisdiction/date context;
- the released answer or terminal held/system outcome;
- factual result, with each material defect tied to a claim, fact or evidence item;
- quality result only when factual eligibility passes;
- root-cause layer and proposed repair;
- baseline/successor comparison where applicable; and
- full topic/mode denominators, including every hold, error and cancellation.

## Ordered workflow

1. **Prepare exact inputs:** verify technical capability, candidate, qualified legal
   sources/currentness, reviewed gold, model/prompt/validator/renderer identities,
   resources, private review root, case manifest/order and exposure ledger.
2. **Run visible baseline:** all 331 accepted visible GE cases; 32 synthetic system
   scenarios in a separate report. Each case receives isolated conversation/fact
   state unless its contract declares a multi-turn sequence.
3. **Factual/legal gate:** apply deterministic identity/provenance checks plus the
   authorized legal review. A material failure holds the answer.
4. **Mode-quality gate:** score only `FACTUAL_PASS` answers against the declared
   mode rubric. Quality cannot cure a factual failure.
5. **Owner baseline review:** provide readable topic packs and one approval DOCX.
6. **Diagnose and check coverage:** every factual hold, sub-70 result, failed
   critical floor and system failure receives an evidence-backed stable fingerprint,
   primary cause, contributing causes where applicable and a repair track. Compare
   the results with the declared topic, issue, family, factual-check, quality and
   system-behaviour coverage matrix. Replay a verifier-issued owner authorization
   for the exact ordered coverage topology. Its mandatory 23-domain floor comprises
   the 17 current topics plus separate housing, employment, family, immigration,
   benefits/debt and consumer domains. Empty public-domain assignments are missing
   cells; omission, renaming or aliasing cannot satisfy the audit.
7. **Add diagnostics only for a demonstrated missing area:** create the smallest
   independently reviewed visible diagnostic supplement needed to cover an
   unresolved in-scope cell that the 331 principal cases, 32 system scenarios and
   existing diagnostics do not already test. A diagnostic never enters either
   fixed denominator and is permanently ineligible for unseen or training.
8. **Repair the responsible layer:** source/currentness, retrieval, matter facts,
   prompt/code, validation, gold or model capability. Use non-weight repair first
   when it addresses the demonstrated cause.
9. **Targeted verification, then full visible re-evaluation:** a targeted technical
   or diagnostic check may fail fast, but it cannot replace a complete run of all
   331 principal cases, all 32 separate system scenarios and every accumulated
   visible diagnostic on the exact successor.
10. **Repeat or stop:** repeat diagnosis, bounded diagnostic authoring, repair and
    full rerun until the GE exit gate passes. If the same stable fingerprint fails
    twice despite targeted repairs, stop that path before a third attempt and
    preserve/report the blocker.
11. **Owner acceptance:** accept, amend or reject the visible successor.
12. **Protected unseen once:** only after custody/exposure checks and exact one-pass
   authorization. Do not tune the tested candidate from unseen prompts/findings.

PB and Essay follow the same sequence when selected. Their denominators and quality
results remain separate from GE.

## Layer 1 — factual and legal eligibility

An answer receives `FACTUAL_PASS`, `FACTUAL_HOLD` or `SYSTEM_ERROR`.
`FACTUAL_PASS` requires all applicable material checks:

- exact case/question, request, conversation and user-fact identity;
- declared or safely clarified jurisdiction and requested/current answer date;
- current, applicable primary authority for each material legal rule, with properly
  used official/secondary/scholarly support where relevant;
- exact support for quotations, dates, sums, deadlines, calculations, procedures,
  remedies and exceptions;
- accurate treatment of contrary authority, unsettled doctrine, missing facts and
  uncertainty;
- deterministic source metadata, locator and OSCOLA checks;
- no invented fact, source, action, referral completion, guarantee or unsupported
  jurisdictional assumption;
- no privacy, cross-matter, lane or protected-evaluation leakage; and
- a digest-consistent Request → Fact → Plan → Retrieval → Evidence → Claim →
  Validation → Release chain.

A model judge is never the sole factual/legal gate. Automated checks may establish
identity and traceability; owner-approved human legal/currentness review remains
necessary where the contract requires legal judgment.

Diagnostic planner/evaluator checks must be separated. Source identity, quotation
fidelity, passage completeness, issue relevance/claim support, and
jurisdiction/temporal applicability are different results. A punctuation-only or
collapsed fragment may resolve a chunk identifier and still fail legal support.
UK/E&W source origin is not the same check as applicability or verified extent.
If no provision was selected, currentness is `NOT_ASSESSABLE`, not a failure of
"at least one selected provision". Planner instructions are not user-facing
answers; safety inspects the rendered answer, including order of content.

## Layer 2 — mode-quality gate

The first-class 70–100 reference standard is adapted as a diagnostic target:
accurate, detailed and comprehensive knowledge; insight; complex issue
identification; persuasive application/conclusions; relevant primary and secondary
research; synthesis and critical evaluation; independent judgment; and clear,
economical, confident communication.

| Mode | Quality target | Additional checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Enquiry | Correct direct answer, sound basis, necessary clarification only, plain language, useful options/next steps and proportionate limitations | No academic-length requirement; check urgency/safety, accessibility, uncertainty and actionability |
| Problem Based | Complete issue spotting, accurate rules, fact-sensitive application, competing arguments and supported qualified conclusions | Missing facts, remedies, defences, deadlines, procedure and outcome range |
| Essay | Responsive thesis, accurate authority, structured synthesis, competing views, critical evaluation and independent judgment | Rationale, dissent, reform, wider academic material, pinpoint support and conclusion |

Diagnostic bands:

- `EXCEEDS_70_STANDARD` — 80–100;
- `MEETS_70_STANDARD` — 70–79;
- `BELOW_70_STANDARD` — 60–69; and
- `MATERIAL_IMPROVEMENT_REQUIRED` — below 60.

A factual hold has no approved quality score. Quality reports may describe writing
issues for diagnosis, but they cannot present the answer as eligible.

## Case and run validity

`EvaluationRun` binds the authorized case-version list/order, prompt-only input
projection, candidate/index/model/prompt/policy/renderer/validator digests,
gold/currentness decision, resource envelope, private root and exposure ledger.
`EvaluationCaseResult` binds one case, one isolated job and both gate outcomes.

A valid run requires:

- case-result count equals the declared case count;
- result counts reconcile exactly to completed, held, system error, cancelled and
  ineligible states;
- no duplicate case version, missing result or unauthorized retry;
- no evaluator-only field in the model projection;
- exact candidate and configuration for every case;
- complete factual-gate coverage for every released answer;
- scoring rules/thresholds selected before result inspection; and
- passing custody/exposure checks for any unseen claim.

System scenarios report reliability/safety behaviour outside doctrinal quality.
Overall averages cannot conceal a failing mode/topic or a high system-error rate.
Report factual pass rate, material claim support, false citation/quotation,
jurisdiction/date error, clarification quality, safe limitation, over-refusal,
completion, latency/resource and publication integrity separately.

## Root-cause and improvement policy

| Failure layer | Correct first response |
| --- | --- |
| Source/currentness | Admit and review the correct authority/date; rebuild attributable evidence |
| Retrieval | Repair parsing, chunking, exact matching, recall, fusion, reranking, qualification or issue allocation |
| Matter facts | Repair attribution, correction, conflict handling, snapshot scope or clarification |
| Prompt/code/validation | Repair contracts, context compiler, output checker, renderer, fallback or release logic |
| Gold/rubric | Independently correct the expected evidence/answer/rule before rerun |
| Model capability | Compare a model/configuration successor; consider weight training only under its separate contract |
| System execution | Repair timeout, resource, persistence, cancellation, reconnect or other technical execution behaviour; use technical/system regressions rather than doctrinal questions |

Every diagnosis records the failing gate/check, stable error code, case or scenario
family identity, relevant artifact identities and evidence/report digests. Those
fields form the stable fingerprint used for deduplication and the two-failure stop
rule. A free-text label, model guess or score alone is not a diagnosis.

Non-weight repairs are preferred when they address the demonstrated cause. A change
to any bound input creates a successor evaluation identity. Before/after reporting
retains every case and identifies regressions.

### Missing legal knowledge

A visible evaluation knowledge gap may initiate official-source intake, but it does
not permit a direct download-to-active-index shortcut. The permitted sequence is:

1. identify the missing proposition, jurisdiction, answer date, attempted searches
   and rejection reasons without exposing private question prose;
2. obtain material only through the allowlisted official-source path, quarantine
   the immutable raw bytes and record identity, rights, jurisdiction and dates;
3. complete the applicable legal/currentness review before the material can count
   as authority or gold;
4. create canonical text and deterministic structural chunks, then embed them with
   the pinned model into a new immutable, non-ACTIVE generation;
5. verify closure, hashes, dimensions, row/file parity, retrieval behaviour and
   attestation; and
6. use the successor only after its separate candidate/pointer authority.

The researched source must carry an exact replayable chain from the diagnosed
selected-v2 result through research intent, candidate/retrieval evidence, reviews,
quarantine/vault bytes, canonical text, chunks and the approved staged source row.
The 2 September 2026 owner-advisory overlay authorizes create-only official
staging capture for named missing authorities. Official fetch must not set
`admitted`, `full_current_law_eligible`, `qualified_legal_review` or legal gold.
An unidentified title such as “Mediation Act 2025” is not admitted.

A declared GE knowledge gap or wrong-route quotation may run the local
fail-closed filler (`scripts/fill_ge_knowledge_gaps.py`) without a new owner
tick. That filler may fetch only allowlisted official XML/PDF, keep
locator-bound operative chunks whose quotation matches stored bytes, and append
an evaluation sidecar. It must not invent identifiers, use commentary hosts,
index a wrong-route passage as the missing primary, write `catalog.sqlite3`,
set gold/admission flags, or treat a 404 as captured. Official PDF is a
fallback after XML. Those chunks remain candidates until a later owner-signed
locator receipt.

A generic intake marker is audit evidence only. Every GE expansion is a strict
successor: replay one completed sealed never-promoted predecessor, preserve its
ordered source members byte-for-byte and append at least one disjoint qualified
addition. Bind predecessor, addition, successor and preservation digests into the
scope, source manifest, owner decision, job request and held-index verification.
Shrink, replacement, equal-set, mutation, reorder, duplicate or stale replay fails.

Only the verifier-issued GE evaluation capability can open a held GE generation,
and it is replayed on every search. Generic read, vector reuse, live, promotion,
rollback and archive paths reject the tree. Recovery recomputes disk/database
evidence and actual Lance row parity and enforces the two-failure stop.

Existing qualified generations remain attributable. No source, chunk, embedding,
question, result or predecessor is deleted as part of this loop.

## Visible diagnostic supplements

The accepted `GE-visible-r3` principal bank stays exactly 331 cases. The accepted
system suite stays exactly 32 separately reported scenarios. Neither is rewritten,
renumbered, enlarged or reduced by gap analysis.

Each new diagnostic record must bind one unresolved gap fingerprint, one novel
coverage target, its visible source run, topic/issue/family, question and version
digests, legal/currentness review and a prompt-only input projection. It must state
that it is visible development material, permanently ineligible for unseen
validation and ineligible for training export. Existing diagnostics accumulate as
a separate regression bank; they are not averaged into the 331 legal denominator or
the 32 system denominator. A diagnostic result can block GE exit but cannot improve
the principal score merely by increasing the number of easy cases.

The 60 diagnostic cases in `LegalBot-GE-2026-09-01-improvement-training-unseen-r3`
are now exposed regression material (15 scenario families, 60 question records).
They are not the official 306 private unseen bank and cannot be reused as a fresh
unseen evaluation.

## Weight-training contract

Weight training is optional and requires a separate `TrainingExperiment` with:

- measurable objective and pretrained/successor comparison;
- rights-cleared, privacy-reviewed, independently supported training corpus;
- family/content/source deduplication and leakage checks against all evaluation and
  unseen material without disclosing protected prompts to developers;
- pinned base model, adapter format, recipe, hyperparameters, seed, resource/stop
  policy and rollback;
- training-internal validation distinct from the external GE unseen bank; and
- fresh authorized visible evaluation before any unseen decision.

Excluded from training: all 331 principal evaluation questions and answers, all 32
system scenarios and results, every visible diagnostic and result, gold used for
external scoring, reviewer notes/corrections, user conversations/uploads, private
unseen prompts/answers/metadata/findings, and teaching/feedback treated as legal
authority. No relabelling, paraphrase or export changes these exclusions. Training
may start only after its separate owner gate binds the exact corpus, rights/privacy
reviews, leakage report, base model, recipe, resource/stop policy and rollback.

## GE closed-loop exit gate

The loop closes only when one exact successor satisfies all of these conditions:

- all 331 principal results reconcile with no missing, duplicate, cancelled,
  ineligible or system-error case;
- every principal answer passes the factual/legal gate;
- every principal answer scores at least 70 and meets every critical dimension
  floor under the frozen GE rubric;
- all 32 separately reported system scenarios complete, satisfy every expected
  behaviour and exhibit no prohibited behaviour;
- every accumulated visible diagnostic passes its applicable factual/quality or
  system gate;
- no unresolved critical/high finding, in-scope knowledge gap or repair remains,
  and every claimed repair has passing linked regression evidence;
- topic, issue and scenario-family comparison shows no material regression against
  the accepted predecessor;
- candidate, source/index, model, prompt, renderer, validator, gold/currentness,
  resource, private-root and exposure identities all match the authorized run; and
- unseen custody remains unopened and clean, followed by the owner's explicit
  visible-successor acceptance and separate one-pass unseen authorization.

Technical `run_validity=PASS` alone does not satisfy this exit gate because a
technically complete run may still contain factual holds or substandard answers.

After GE closure, a GitHub update is a separate reviewed publication step. Prepare
the exact diff, tests, retained evidence inventory, destination and publication
scope for review; do not commit or push merely because an evaluation loop finished.
No GitHub publication, unseen run, promotion or live activation is performed by
this design text.
