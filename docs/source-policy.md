# Source and rights policy

| Lane | Examples | Legal authority in answers |
|---|---|---|
| Primary authority | Cases, legislation, rules | Yes, after identity/currentness verification |
| Official secondary | Government and regulator guidance | Yes, labelled by legal status |
| Scholarship | Licensed journals and books | Persuasive analysis only |
| Private teaching | Lectures, tutorials and seminars | Issue spotting only; never independent authority |
| Assessment guidance | Marker comments and rubrics | Writing guidance only |

Rephrasing never removes provenance or creates new ownership. Private supplied material remains local. Raw weak student answers are excluded from runtime evidence and training exports. Explicit 70+ feedback yields positive assessment rules; 60–69 and 50–59 feedback yields anti-patterns and remediation rules after human review.

An explicit source notice prohibiting artificial-intelligence training,
language-model response generation or comparable model use overrides any prior
teaching approval. That representation is rejected from every model-facing
index and prompt view. Teaching material with permitted use is physically
isolated, contributes only fixed issue-taxonomy/query-expansion signals, and is
never cited as authority. Derived index text is PII-scrubbed; immutable raw and
canonical source representations remain unchanged.

Online full text is staged only where source-specific rights permit it. Find Case Law remains metadata/link-only until a computational-analysis licence is recorded. FCA, PRA, systematic SRA content and unlicensed publisher copies are metadata-only or quarantined. An owner-supplied subscription export may be indexed only when the owner explicitly authorises that exact local collection and the copy records an applicable private educational-use basis. It remains local-only scholarship, never primary authority, and cannot enter a training export, cloud service or shared corpus. This exception does not authorise automated retrieval from the subscription service.

Legislation.gov.uk “latest available” XML is stored as an immutable
`latest_available_revised_snapshot`. It is not described as a verified
point-in-time consolidation merely because it has a download date. For a
material E&W proposition, provision extent, commencement and relevant unapplied
effects must be checked; otherwise the answer is explicitly limited.
