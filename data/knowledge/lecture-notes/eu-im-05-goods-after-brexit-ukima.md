## Withdrawal Agreement: the Protocol on Ireland/Northern Ireland
- **Article 5(5).** Northern Ireland remains within the EU customs regime and single-market rules for goods.
- Articles 30 and 110 TFEU apply in respect of Northern Ireland.
- **The Windsor Framework (2023)** revised the operation of the Protocol, including the "green lane" for goods staying in Northern Ireland and the Stormont brake.

## Trade and Cooperation Agreement (an Article 217 TFEU association agreement)
- Goods no longer move freely.
- Tariffs and quotas are zero for goods meeting the rules of origin.
- Customs formalities, regulatory checks and sanitary and phytosanitary controls now apply.

## UK Internal Market Act 2020
- **Purpose.** The Act creates a UK internal market for goods, services and professional qualifications across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. It partly codifies Article 34 case law.
- **Section 3: mutual recognition.**
- Based on the Cassis de Dijon principle.
- Applies to product requirements: statutory rules on the characteristics, presentation or production of goods.
- **Section 4: non-discrimination.**
- Based on Keck.
- Applies to manner-of-sale requirements. Direct and indirect discrimination against goods from another part of the UK is prohibited.
- **Section 8(6).** Indirect discrimination can be justified only on the grounds of human, animal or plant life or health, or public safety or security.
- **Section 10 and Schedule 1.** The market access principles do not apply to measures against a serious threat to health from pests, diseases or unsafe food.
- **Debates.**
- Centralisation of regulatory power in Westminster, with limits on devolved policy choices.
- Scope: the Act covers statutory requirements only.
- Enforcement: the principles have no "direct legal effect" comparable to EU law.
- Academic commentary exists by Weatherill and by Armstrong.
