## History
- the Peace of Westphalia (1648).

## Harmonisation
- the New York Convention 1958 (arbitration);
- the Model Law on International Commercial Mediation (2018);
- the Singapore Convention on Mediation (2019).
- **Regional instruments:** the EU Mediation Directive 2008/52 and the US Uniform Mediation Act.

## Private international law
- soft-law mediator standards and rules inform the interpretation of the Singapore Convention;
- recognition and enforcement of cross-border mediated settlements runs through the Singapore Convention.
