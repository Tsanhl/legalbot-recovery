## Standards of conduct
- **Key codes.** The US Model Standards of Conduct and the European Code of Conduct for Mediators.
- **Singapore Convention link.** A serious breach of standards that affected the settlement is a ground for refusing relief (Article 5(1)(e)–(f)).

## Liability of mediators
- Tapoohi v Lewenberg (No 2) [2003] VSC 410, where a mediator's liability was held arguable;
- Clay v Lenkiewicz Foundation;
- Sejane v CCMA;
- Kasipersad v CCMA.

## Judges as mediators
- The EU Mediation Directive excludes settlement attempts by the judge seised of the case.
