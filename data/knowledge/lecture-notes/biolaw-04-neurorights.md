## Legal developments
- **Chile.** The first constitutional neurorights amendment (2021), protecting mental privacy, cognitive liberty and identity.
- In Girardi v Emotiv (Supreme Court, 2023), a consumer EEG company collected brain data without adequate consent. The Court ordered deletion and protected mental privacy.
- **California.** SB 1223 (signed 28 September 2024) added "neural data" as sensitive personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act, with rights to know, delete and limit use.
- **EU and Council of Europe.** Debate continues over treating neural data as special category data under the GDPR or creating a new framework, and over neurotechnology guidance under Convention 108+. EU reports indicate that regulation is coming.
- **Latin America.** Mexico (2023 Charter of Digital Rights amendments; a 2024 General Law on Neurorights bill) and Brazil (Rio Grande do Sul constitutional amendment, 2023).
