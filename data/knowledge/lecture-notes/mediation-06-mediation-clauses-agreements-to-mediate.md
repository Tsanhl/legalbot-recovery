## Enforceability issues
- The watershed case is Hooper Bailie v Natcon (NSW, 1992), which enforced a clause defining a certain process.
- Relevant cases: Halsey v Milton Keynes NHS Trust [2004] EWCA Civ 576; Alassini v Telecom Italia (C-317/08 etc), which upheld mandatory pre-litigation settlement attempts.
- Computershare v Perpetual (Vic) granted a stay to enforce an ADR clause even though some procedures were yet to be agreed.
- In England, Cable & Wireless v IBM [2002] EWHC 2059 (Comm) enforced a clause referring to CEDR procedure.

## Compliance and good faith
- **Good faith.** Giles J in Elizabeth Bay Developments v Boral (NSW, 1995) noted the tension between self-interested negotiation and good faith. Bad faith or unreasonableness is easier to show, sometimes without piercing confidentiality.

## International instruments
- **The Directive and the Convention.** Neither the EU Mediation Directive nor the Singapore Convention covers the enforcement of mediation clauses.
- **The UNCITRAL Model Law** implicitly recognises such clauses in Article 14: if parties agree not to litigate during mediation, courts and tribunals give effect to that, subject to necessary interim measures.

## Duty to advise on mediation
- **England.** The CPR and costs sanctions apply. Halsey says lawyers should routinely consider ADR with their clients.
- **Elsewhere.** Some jurisdictions impose a statutory duty (Ireland, Mediation Act 2017). A South African court sanctioned lawyers personally in costs (Brownlee v Brownlee).

## Agreements to mediate (the contract for a specific mediation)
- **Case law.** Policy in England favours enforcement (Beauty Star v Janmohamed). See also Universal Satspace v Republic of Kenya.
