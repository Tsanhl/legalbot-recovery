## Map of homicide
- corporate manslaughter (Corporate Manslaughter and Corporate Homicide Act 2007);
- causing death by dangerous driving (Road Traffic Act 1988, s 1), by careless driving (s 2B), or by careless driving while under the influence (s 3A);
- infanticide (Infanticide Act 1938);
- child destruction (Infant Life (Preservation) Act 1929).
