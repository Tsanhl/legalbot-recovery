## Neurolaw
- Examples: R v Halling [2012] EWCA Crim 1774 (brain injury) and R v X (childhood amnesia) [2005] EWCA Crim 1828 (reliability of testimony).
- Example: Roper v Simmons, 543 US 551 (2005) abolished juvenile capital punishment, relying on adolescent psychological immaturity.
