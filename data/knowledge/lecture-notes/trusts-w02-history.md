# How equity produced the trust
- The Earl of Oxford's Case (1615) and the Crown's 1616 endorsement confirmed that equity prevails where the two conflict.
- Equity is not unlimited discretion: courts reject the idea that it varies "like the Chancellor's foot" (Gee v Pritchard (1818)). Unconscionability remains an organising idea in modern cases such as Pitt v Holt [2013] UKSC 26 and Pennington v Waine [2002] EWCA Civ 227.
