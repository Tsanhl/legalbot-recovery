## Why mediation
- **What parties want.** Chief Justice Burger's point in "Our Vicious Legal Spiral" (1977) is that people with legal problems, like people in pain, want relief quickly and cheaply, not the ceremony of the courtroom.

## Definitions
- EU Mediation Directive 2008/52, Article 3: a structured process, however named, in which two or more parties attempt by themselves, voluntarily, to reach a settlement with the assistance of a mediator. The process may be initiated by the parties, suggested or ordered by a court, or prescribed by law.
- UNCITRAL Model Law on International Commercial Mediation (2018) and the Singapore Convention (2019): a process, whatever it is called, in which parties attempt an amicable settlement with the help of a third person who lacks authority to impose a solution.
