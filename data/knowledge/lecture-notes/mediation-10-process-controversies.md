## 3. Public interest
- "The Vanishing Trial" (ABA, 2004): the US federal civil trial rate fell from 11.5% (1962) to 1.8% (2002), while filings rose fivefold. The causes are multiple: cost and complexity, risk aversion, managerial judging and ADR.
- The 2011 Cornell, Straus and CPR survey of Fortune 1000 counsel suggests that mediation has displaced arbitration rather than trials.

## 5. Symbiosis with adjudication (a concluding frame)
- **Integration.** Systems now build settlement in at appropriate points: CPR costs sanctions in England, mandatory pre-trial mediation in parts of the US and Australia, and the EU Mediation Directive.
- The courts now sponsor settlement, and the architect of the CPR (Lord Woolf) accepted that justice can be achieved through settlement.
