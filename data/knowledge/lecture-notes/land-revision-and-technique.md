## Registered-land problem checklist
- How does the LRA 2002 treat it: a registrable disposition? ss 28–29?
- If not, is it overriding (Sch 3), and with what effect?

## Critical themes to prepare
- Whether proprietary estoppel is too flexible, and what Guest v Guest clarified or left open.
- Whether Wheeldon v Burrows is still needed alongside s 62, and the Law Commission's easement reforms.
