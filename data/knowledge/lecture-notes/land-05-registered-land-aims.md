# Registered land: what registration is for
- In registered land the title (estate), not the land, is recorded at HM Land Registry under a unique title number, with the owner and the interests affecting it (LRA 2002; Land Registration Rules 2003). One parcel may carry several titles (freehold and leasehold). The system is title-based; neither the Land Charges Act nor the doctrine of notice applies.
